# Legal Notice

NameGuard aggregates publicly available trademark-register, domain-registry,
and web data to help users assess brand-name risk.

## Not legal advice

Results are informational indicators only. They do not constitute legal
advice, a freedom-to-operate opinion, or a guarantee of registrability.
Trademark law is jurisdiction-specific and fact-dependent; consult a
qualified intellectual-property professional before relying on any result
for a commercial naming decision.

## Data sources

- Trademark data is retrieved from public register interfaces and may be
  incomplete, delayed, or unavailable for some jurisdictions.
- Domain data reflects registry responses at query time and may change at
  any moment.
- Web-presence results are snapshots of public search results.

## Accuracy

A "green" result does not prove a name is safe; a "red" result does not
prove infringement. Sources can fail or be unavailable — the report always
shows which sources actually answered.
