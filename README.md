# NameGuard

**Check a brand name before you commit to it.**

NameGuard is a desktop app that gives founders and creators a clear,
plain-English answer to the question "can I actually use this name?" —
before the domain is bought, the logo is made, and the paperwork is filed.

## What it checks

- **Trademark registers** — filings across dozens of jurisdictions, with
  live/dead status, owner, and goods-and-services classes.
- **Domain availability** — `.com`, `.co.uk`, `.uk`, `.net`, `.io`, plus
  signals about whether a taken domain is actually in use or just parked.
- **Web presence** — what the internet already associates with that name.

## What you get

A single risk score with a plain-English breakdown:

- **Green** — low apparent conflict
- **Amber** — worth a closer look
- **Red** — likely conflict; pick a safer name

Every result shows exactly which sources were checked, so "nothing found"
is never confused with "couldn't check".

## Important

NameGuard is a research indicator, **not legal advice**. Trademark risk is a
legal judgement — for high-stakes names, consult a qualified IP attorney.
